Novus Automation designs custom AI agents that take the repetitive work off your team's plate — so your people spend their time on strategy, clients and growth instead of spreadsheets.

Our story

Novus Automation was built inside a working marketing agency, not a lab. We kept watching skilled strategists lose their week to keyword exports, outreach follow-ups and content production — work that was necessary but repetitive. So we built our own agents to handle it.

SAGE, NOVA and SCRIBE were designed, tested and refined against real client workloads before we offered them to anyone else. Everything we ship has already earned its place in a live agency's day-to-day — which is why it solves the real version of each problem, not a demo of it.
