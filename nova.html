Meet NOVA

NOVA is the AI sales automation agent that fills your agency's pipeline — finding qualified leads, personalising outreach, running the follow-ups and keeping your CRM honest, so warm calls land on the calendar without anyone copy-pasting.

What is NOVA?

NOVA is an AI sales automation agent for marketing agencies. It identifies qualified leads, personalises outreach at scale, manages follow-ups, books meetings and updates your CRM — turning a leaky, manual pipeline into a system that runs itself.

Most agencies are good at the work and bad at the chase. Deals die in the gap between a first reply and the third follow-up nobody sent. NOVA is sales automation built for that gap: it does the repetitive outreach and pipeline admin so your people spend their energy on the conversations that close.

Capabilities

What NOVA automates

Lead identification & scoring

NOVA finds and prioritises the prospects most likely to become retainer clients, so your team spends outreach time on the accounts that are actually worth it.

Personalised outreach at scale

Drafts and sends outreach that reads like a human wrote it for that one prospect — personalised from real signals, not a mail-merge blast — across email and your channels.

Automated follow-ups

The leaky part of most agency pipelines is follow-through. NOVA runs the second, third and fourth touch on schedule so warm leads never go cold because nobody had time.

Meeting booking

Routes interested prospects to your calendar and books qualified calls, so your closers walk into conversations that are already warm.

CRM hygiene

Logs every touch, updates deal stages and keeps your CRM honest automatically — no more pipeline reviews built on stale data.

Pipeline reporting

Shows what is moving, what is stuck and where deals stall, so you can forecast and fix the funnel instead of guessing.
