Meet NOVA

Your AI sales automation agent. NOVA keeps the pipeline full and the follow-ups moving, so your team closes retainers instead of chasing them.

What is NOVA?

NOVA is an AI sales automation agent for marketing agencies. It identifies qualified leads, personalises outreach at scale, manages follow-ups, books meetings and keeps your CRM current — turning a leaky manual process into a steady, measurable pipeline.

Your team stops copy-pasting outreach and updating spreadsheets, and starts showing up to calls that are already warm.

Lead identification
