Glossary — AI SEO

What is
AI SEO?

AI SEO means using artificial intelligence to do SEO work at scale — and optimizing to be cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Here's the definition and how to use it.

AI SEO is the use of artificial intelligence to do search engine optimization work — keyword research, content optimization, technical audits, rank tracking and reporting — faster and at larger scale than manual methods. It also increasingly means optimizing to be cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, not just ranked by classic search.

Key takeaways

  • AI SEO means using AI to do SEO work — research, optimization, audits, tracking — at scale.
  • It now also covers AI-search visibility: being mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers.
  • AI handles research and analysis; humans keep strategy, editorial judgement and E-E-A-T.
  • For agencies, an AI SEO agent removes the operating load across every client.

The two meanings of "AI SEO"

First, using AI to do SEO: machine learning that clusters keywords by intent, scores drafts against ranking pages, finds technical issues, forecasts movement and assembles reports. Second, SEO for AI: structuring content so AI answer engines understand and cite it, since more searches now end in an AI-generated answer than a list of blue links. A modern approach covers both.

What AI does well in SEO — and what it doesn't

AI is excellent at the high-volume, pattern-heavy parts: research, clustering, auditing, tracking and first-draft briefs. It is not a substitute for strategy, a genuine point of view, first-hand experience or editorial judgement — the signals of experience and expertise (E-E-A-T) that both Google and AI engines increasingly reward. The winning pattern is AI for the volume, humans for the judgement.

How to use AI for SEO

  1. Research: cluster keywords by intent and read the live SERP (see SAGE).
  2. Brief: turn research into outline-level briefs with gap analysis.
  3. Produce: draft against the brief, then have a human add originality and the edit (see AI content agents).
  4. Audit & track: automate technical checks and rank monitoring.
  5. Report: unify Search Console and GA4 into client-ready output.
