Meet SAGE

SAGE is the AI SEO agent that runs the research, auditing and reporting that eats your team's week — so your strategists spend their time on strategy, not spreadsheets.

What is SAGE?

SAGE is an AI SEO research and intelligence agent for marketing agencies. It runs keyword research, technical site audits, content briefs, AI Overview gap analysis, rank tracking and cannibalization checks — then pulls Google Search Console and GA4 into one view and turns it all into client-ready SEO proposals as PDF.

Most agencies run SEO across a dozen disconnected tools and tabs. SAGE is SEO automation that does the heavy lifting as a single agent: you ask, and it hands back research-grade output in minutes — ready to put in front of a client. It is built for the way agencies actually work, across many clients at once, not for a single in-house site.

Capabilities

What SAGE automates

Keyword & SERP research

Surfaces the terms worth targeting, maps search intent and reads the live SERP so every brief is built against what actually ranks today — not last year's playbook.

Technical site audits

Crawls each client site for the crawl, indexing, speed and structure issues that quietly hold rankings back, then returns a prioritised, plain-English fix list your developers can action.

Content briefs & gap analysis

Builds outline-level content briefs and flags the AI Overview, topical and keyword gaps your competitors already own, so writers start from a plan instead of a blank page.

Rank tracking

Monitors keyword positions over time across every client so you can show movement in a report and catch a drop before the client emails you about it.

Cannibalization checks

Finds pages competing for the same intent so you can consolidate, redirect and stop splitting your own authority across near-duplicate URLs.

GSC + GA4 in one view

Pulls Google Search Console and GA4 into a single report and exports a client-ready SEO proposal as PDF — research and reporting stop living in a dozen browser tabs.
