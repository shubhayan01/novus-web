Meet SAGE

Your AI SEO research agent. SAGE handles the research, auditing and reporting that eats your team's week, so strategists spend their time on strategy.

What is SAGE?

SAGE is an AI SEO research and intelligence agent for marketing agencies. It runs keyword research, technical site audits, content briefs, AI Overview gap analysis, rank tracking and cannibalization checks — then pulls Google Search Console and GA4 into one view and turns it all into client-ready SEO proposals as PDF.

Instead of juggling a dozen tools and tabs, your team asks SAGE and gets research-grade output in minutes — ready to put in front of a client.

Capabilities

What SAGE automates

Keyword & SERP research

Surfaces the terms worth targeting, maps intent and reads the live SERP so you brief against what actually ranks.

Technical site audits

Crawls for the issues that hold rankings back and returns a prioritised, plain-English fix list.

Content briefs & gaps

Builds outline-level briefs and flags AI Overview and topical gaps your competitors already own.

Rank tracking

Monitors positions over time so you can show movement and catch drops before the client does.

Cannibalization checks

Finds pages competing for the same intent so you can consolidate and stop splitting your own authority.

GSC + GA4, one view

Pulls Search Console and GA4 into a single report and exports a client-ready SEO proposal as PDF.
