Use Cases

Where agencies put
Novus agents to work

SAGE, NOVA and SCRIBE were built inside a working agency. These are the day-to-day workflows they take off your team's plate.

Pitch-ready SEO audits in minutes

SAGE runs the keyword research, site audit and gap analysis, then exports a client-ready proposal as PDF — so new-business audits stop eating a strategist's afternoon.

Monthly client reporting on autopilot

Pull Google Search Console and GA4 into one view, track rankings over time and surface movement before the client asks about it.

Qualified outbound without an SDR

NOVA identifies the right prospects, personalises outreach at scale, handles follow-ups and keeps your CRM clean — turning cold lists into booked demos.

Briefs to drafts, same day

SCRIBE turns a brief into a structured draft that holds tone and quality, so editors spend their time on polish instead of a blank page.

Scale output without new hires

Take on more retainers by letting the agents absorb the repetitive research, reporting and production work that usually forces a headcount decision.

One source of truth per client

Research, rankings, content status and pipeline live in one place, so the whole team works from the same picture instead of a dozen tabs.
