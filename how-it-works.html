How Novus
Automation works

From first call to live agents, we handle the heavy lifting. Here is exactly how Novus takes SAGE, NOVA and SCRIBE from a conversation to running inside your agency — done-for-you, start to finish.

You tell us the workflow that's slowing your team down. We build, connect and run the agent that removes it.

The process

Five steps to live

01

Discovery

A short call to map the workflows eating your team's time and decide which agent — SAGE, NOVA or SCRIBE — removes the most friction first.

02

Build & configure

We set the agent up around your processes, voice and outputs — not a generic template. You review, we refine.

03

Integrate

We connect the agent to your existing stack — Google Search Console, GA4, your CRM and more — and confirm every integration before you commit.

04

Launch

The agent goes live, usually within days of kickoff. We onboard your team so day one is productive, not confusing.

05

Support & optimise

We monitor, tune and extend the agent as your agency scales. Month-to-month, no lock-in — add or remove agents any time.
